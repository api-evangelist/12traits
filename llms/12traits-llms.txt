# Solsten

> Solsten is a psychological-AI company that provides audience intelligence for product, marketing, and research teams. It combines validated psychographic measurement with behavioral data to model how real people think, decide, and engage. Its product suite includes Elaris (audience-aware content generation), Navigator (audience intelligence), and Traits (real customer understanding).

Elaris and Navigator are documented in their own llms.txt files, at https://elaris.new/llms.txt and https://navigator.solsten.io/llms.txt.

---

## Products

- [Elaris](https://elaris.new/): Solsten's generative AI platform for creating and testing audience-aware ad creative and messaging, powered by synthetic "digital twin" audiences built from proprietary, first-party psychological data.
- [Traits](https://solsten.io/traits/): Solsten's core audience-intelligence product that assesses any company's or product's real customers and clusters psychographic profiles into science-backed personas.
- [Navigator](https://navigator.solsten.io/): Audience intelligence platform that allows anyone to identify and deeply understand a demographic's, brand's, or product's current, prospective, and untapped audiences using psychological profiling from first-party data.
